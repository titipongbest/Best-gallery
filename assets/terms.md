## Terms of Service

“Best gallery” is an open-source gallery and metadata explorer app (forked from Aves) allowing you to access and manage your local photos and videos, as well as your optional MEGA cloud drive.

The app is designed for legal, authorized and acceptable purposes.

## Disclaimer

The app is released “as-is”, without any warranty, responsibility or liability. Use of the app is at your own risk.

## Privacy Policy

Best gallery does not collect personal data or send analytics to third parties. We never have access to your photos and videos. This also means that we cannot recover them for you if you delete them without backing them up.

- **Local storage & security**: App lock PINs, MEGA lock PINs, and in-app crash logs are stored only on your device.
- **MEGA cloud (optional)**: If you sign in to MEGA, the app communicates directly with MEGA servers to browse, search, rename, and download your files. Downloaded files are kept in app-private storage on your device until you clear the cache or log out, and are excluded from system backups.
- **Installed apps (optional)**: With your consent, the app accesses the inventory of installed apps to improve album display.
